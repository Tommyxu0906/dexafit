FAQ

How it works.

A few of the questions teams asked during the concept review.

How are partners vetted?
Every partner submits credentials (CSCS, RD, DPT, PharmD, USAT, etc.), undergoes a methodology interview, and signs an outcomes agreement tied to client retest results. Reviews come from verified DexaFit members only.
What data drives the recommendations?
DEXA (lean mass, fat mass, regional asymmetry, visceral fat, bone density), RMR, VO₂ Max, lab panels when shared, plus client-stated goals, weekly time budget, support preference, and location. Re-ranks automatically after every retest.
How does DexaFit benefit?
Three revenue lines: partner referral fees, exclusive member-rate retest packages, and upgraded recurring memberships. Plus the retention moat — clients who pair a scan with a pathway return 3× more often than scan-only clients.
Is supplement recommendation regulated-safe?
All supplement recommendations are framed as audits delivered by licensed pharmacists or RDs, with sourcing and disclaimers. No DexaFit-branded SKU push, no commissions on individual supplements — the value sits in the audit itself.
Can clients opt out of partner matching?
Yes. The Hub also offers a self-guided track with progress templates and a pre-booked retest. Matching is a feature, not a paywall — the report itself is always accessible.
